Kurt Urfel'sMIDI Fixerfor Space Station 14 instruments

Finds and fixes MIDIs whose drums turn into a piano or guitar on the DAW, and MIDIs that play too quietly. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded.

Why the drums turn into a piano

    

      
    
        
  1. The DAW and the super synthesizer allow program changes. Then SS14 passes every bank select (CC0) straight to the synth with _synth.BankSelect(channel, value) in RobustToolbox's MidiRenderer.cs. That includes channel 10, the drum channel.
    2. 
        
  2. Lots of MIDIs send CC0 = 0 and then a program on channel 10, like 0 or 25. FluidSynth looks in bank 0 and finds a normal instrument: 0 is the piano, 25 the steel guitar. It only falls back to drums when bank 0 has nothing there.
    3. 
        
  3. SS14 does not reset the synth between songs (player.reset-synth = 0). One broken song can break the next, clean one.
    4. 
        
  4. The fix removes CC0/CC32 on channel 10 and sets bank 120 at the start. GeneralUser GS keeps the same drum kits there as in bank 128. Normal players ignore it or read it as the GM2 drum bank, so it does no harm anywhere else.
    5. 
        
  5. Volume: velocity, channel volume (CC7) and expression (CC11) all turn the sound down. Boost raises all three together until the loudest notes hit full level. SS14 cannot play a file louder than that. Past that point, raise Options → Audio → MIDI (Instrument) Volume in SS14. Every listener sets that slider for themselves.
    6. 
        
  6. The preview runs the same synth, FluidSynth, in your browser and feeds it the MIDI the way MidiRenderer does. “Intended” plays the file the way a normal GM/GS player would.
